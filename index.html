About Us

We Are Protecting The Ocean With The Power Of AI

DeepSea Guardian is an advanced AI platform that combines underwater drones, satellites, sonar, and IoT sensors to continuously monitor ocean health, detect threats in real time, and protect marine ecosystems for future generations.

Our Mission

Protect marine biodiversity using advanced AI technology.

Our Vision

Create a healthier ocean for future generations.

Our Impact

Building a global community for ocean protection.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can researchers access the data?

Verified marine biologists and research institutions can apply for API access through our Partnerships portal.

Is the platform open source?

Select machine learning models and dataset schemas are open-sourced on our GitHub to encourage community contributions.

How do I report an environmental anomaly?

Users can submit reports via the 'Contact Form' on this page, and our AI will triage the data against satellite imagery.
